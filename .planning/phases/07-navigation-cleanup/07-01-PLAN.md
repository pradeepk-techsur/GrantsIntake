## Implementation Notes

- Both Awards and Monitoring items in the grantor sidebar were gated behind `isGrantorAdminOrOfficer`. The nav items are removed entirely — routes remain intact in App.tsx (no backend changes required, no routes deleted).
- The applicant sidebar Awards item pointed to `/applicant/workspaces` (the workspace list). The item label "Awards" was misleading since workspaces represent in-progress applications, not awarded grants. The route `/applicant/workspaces` remains accessible via direct URL and from within the application flow; it is simply not listed in the top-level navigation.
- No backend changes. No route deletions. UI-only change.
- WCAG 2.1 AA unaffected — remaining nav items retain aria-label, aria-current, and focus management.
